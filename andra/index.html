02 / Artist file

Music for weird little creatures.

Andra is the electronic music project of producer, performer, DJ, and audio engineer Drew Dale.

The project moves between detailed sound design, downtempo ambience, melancholic electronics, and club music. Some tracks began years before they were finished. Others feel like the soundtrack to a water level that was removed from the final game.

Across releases and performances, experimentation stays connected to melody, emotion, and the occasional overly specific piece of lore.
