Max/MSP · Motion tracking · Interactive instrument

The Kinected DJ

I built a performance instrument that maps full body motion to audio effects during a DJ set. An Xbox Kinect sends joint positions and hand states through KinectV2-OSC to Max, where I can calibrate movement and route it to effect parameters. This iteration combines Rekordbox and a DDJ-400 with a Max effect patcher containing 48 selectable effects.

Input
Kinect body tracking via OSC
Mapping
Calibrated movement to effect parameters
Output
Processed DJ audio

MATLAB · Digital signal processing · Experimental synthesis

Concatenative timbre synthesis

A prototype that segments source recordings into grains, compares Bark-spectrum and harmonic-ratio features with a target, then reassembles selected grains with overlap-add. The repository includes the original coursework and a documented revision; the revision has been reviewed but not run in MATLAB.

Fauna of Mirrors III

I designed a Max patch to extend FUSE Ensemble’s performance. It plays stereo tape to the main outputs while routing a synchronized click track to separate performer headphone outputs, supporting coordinated playback during the piece.

MIDI synthesizer

A self-contained Max instrument built around MIDI note input, selectable waveforms, timbre controls, an ADSR envelope, portamento, vibrato, tremolo, and chord intervals. The patch includes its subpatchers and generated wave tables.
